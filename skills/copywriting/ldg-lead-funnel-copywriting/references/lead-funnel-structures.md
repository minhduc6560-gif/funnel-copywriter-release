# Lead Funnel Structures

Every creation run must produce all three structures below as three complete landing-page versions. They share the same approved audience, value exchange, facts, CTA, and brand voice. Do not replace them with three angles inside one structure.

## Version 1 - Landing Page Tối Giản

Required order:

1. Headline.
2. Subheadline.
3. Registration form.

The form block must contain only:

- Form Heading.
- Các trường: Approved minimum fields.
- CTA button copy.

Use concise copy. Do not add benefit grids, founder stories, FAQs, or unrelated sections.

Completion criterion: the audience, promised value, and registration action are clear without relying on another version.

## Version 2 - Landing Page Ngắn Có Lợi Ích

Required order:

1. Headline.
2. Subheadline.
3. Three to five benefit bullets explaining the gift's value, importance, or practical use.
4. A short registration prompt.
5. Registration form and CTA.
6. Lead-magnet image placement/handoff note.

Benefit bullets must describe reader outcomes, decisions, or useful applications - not merely repeat a table of contents.

The image note states what approved asset is needed and where it belongs. This skill does not create the image or visual prompt.

Completion criterion: this is a complete short page with 3-5 distinct benefits and a clear form CTA.

## Version 3 - Landing Page Nhiều Section

Required order:

1. **Section 1 - Hero:** headline, subheadline, form, CTA.
2. **Section 2 - Persuasion:** choose either an approved storytelling section or a benefit section based on the available source material.
3. **Section 3 - Company/Expert Profile:** approved introduction, credentials, authority, and relevance to the resource.
4. Optional final CTA only when it improves flow without adding unsupported claims.

If no approved story, profile, or credential exists, use `[CẦN BỔ SUNG: ...]` rather than inventing one.

Completion criterion: every section answers a different question and the page includes both persuasion and approved authority context.

## Shared Copy Rules

### Headlines

Connect the audience, a specific problem or useful outcome, the lead-magnet format, and an approved mechanism where possible.

### CTA

Describe the immediate exchange accurately, such as:

- `Nhận checklist`.
- `Tải tài liệu`.
- `Nhận bộ template`.

Avoid generic CTA copy when a concrete action is known.

### Cross-Version Consistency

All three versions must keep identical core facts. They may differ in depth and presentation, but must not conflict about the gift, audience, delivery behavior, proof, or CTA.
